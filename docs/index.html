Why the wait: demo mode

The server scales to zero when nobody asks. The first visit after a quiet spell waits while Azure pulls a 1.5 GB image with the embedding and OCR models inside and the app loads them: usually about a minute, up to 75 seconds. A production deployment keeps one replica warm; the demo stays inside the free tier instead. Once awake it answers in 3–6 seconds and sleeps again after five idle minutes.

The paper plane is on a 75-second schedule; the stations below it light up on the real signals: the request leaves, the container answers, the app reports its models loaded and the index found on Neon.

The corpus is synthetic: generated documents with a known ground truth, no real company data. Answers are limited to what the documents say; when they do not say it, the demo says so.
